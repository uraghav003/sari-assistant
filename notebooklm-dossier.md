## 4. CREDIT UNDERWRITING HEURISTICS & FORMULAS

### 4.1 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
The FOIR metric determines the maximum permissible monthly repayment capacity of the borrower across all existing and proposed loan EMIs:

$$\text{FOIR (\%)} = \frac{\sum \text{Existing Monthly EMIs} + \text{Proposed Loan EMI}}{\text{Verifiable Net Monthly Income}} \times 100$$

**Permissible FOIR Caps by Income Category:**
- **Tier 1 (Salary < ₹35,000/mo):** Capped at **45% - 50%**
- **Tier 2 (Salary ₹35,000 - ₹75,000/mo):** Capped at **50% - 55%**
- **Tier 3 (Salary ₹75,000 - ₹1,50,000/mo):** Capped at **60% - 65%**
- **Tier 4 (High Net Worth > ₹1,50,000/mo):** Capped at **65% - 70%** (Subject to minimum ₹50,000 monthly living expense buffer)

**Self-Employed Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):**
$$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{PAT} + \text{Depreciation} + \text{Interest on Long-Term Debt}}{\text{Annual Principal Repayments} + \text{Annual Interest Repayments}}$$
*Benchmark:* DSCR must be $\ge 1.35$ for prime private banks; NBFCs accept $\ge 1.15$.

### 4.2 Loan to Value (LTV) Ratios
Governed by RBI regulatory mandates and collateral marketability:
- **Home Loan (Ticket Size $\le$ ₹30 Lakhs):** Up to **90% LTV**
- **Home Loan (Ticket Size ₹30 Lakhs - ₹75 Lakhs):** Up to **80% LTV**
- **Home Loan (Ticket Size > ₹75 Lakhs):** Up to **75% LTV**
- **Loan Against Property (Residential):** Up to **65% - 70% LTV**
- **Loan Against Property (Commercial Office / Retail):** Up to **50% - 60% LTV**
- **Loan Against Property (Industrial Shed / Vacant Plot):** Up to **40% - 50% LTV**

### 4.3 CIBIL & Experian Credit Score Tiers
1. **Tier A (750 - 900) - Prime Sovereign:**
   - Pre-qualified for lowest bank ROI floor (e.g. 8.40% HL, 10.25% PL).
   - Instant processing, zero co-applicant mandate, waiver of administrative inspections.
2. **Tier B (700 - 749) - Standard Retail:**
